1. Overview

Welcome to Business Collections ("we", "our", or "us"). Business Collections is an application designed to integrate with Meta Business Suite to assist businesses in managing customer invoices, collections, payment reminders, catalog integrations, and customer communication workflows.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data and business information processed through our application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information when you connect and use the Business Collections app within Meta Business Suite.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you configure and use Business Collections, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Business name, email address, contact details, user ID, and administrative profile details provided during registration or Meta authentication.
  • Meta Business Suite Data: Connected Business Manager ID, Page IDs, Ad Account IDs, WhatsApp Business Account details, and associated metadata granted via Meta OAuth permissions.
  • Transaction & Collection Data: Invoices, payment transaction history, outstanding balances, order records, customer contact references (names, phone numbers, email addresses) needed for sending collection reminders.
  • Usage & Technical Data: IP addresses, browser types, device information, access timestamps, system logs, and error telemetry.

3. Meta API & Permissions Data

Business Collections accesses specific data via Meta Graph APIs only after you grant explicit permission through Meta OAuth authorization. These permissions may include:

  • pages_show_list / pages_read_engagement: To view and manage connected Meta Pages.
  • business_management: To associate your Meta Business Account with Business Collections.
  • whatsapp_business_messaging / pages_messaging: To automate payment reminder messages and collection updates to customers.

Meta Platform Compliance Guarantee: Business Collections strictly complies with the Meta Platform Terms and Meta Developer Policies. We will never sell, trade, or misuse data obtained through Meta APIs for unauthorized advertising or profiling.

4. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly to fulfill the functional features of the Business Collections application:

  • To synchronize invoice, billing, and collection status with Meta Business Suite channels.
  • To send automated or manual payment reminder messages via Meta messaging channels (WhatsApp/Messenger).
  • To calculate business analytics, outstanding balance reports, and collection progress dashboards.
  • To provide customer support, system updates, and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • To ensure security, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your business or customer personal data to third parties. We share information only under the following limited circumstances:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc.: Data transmitted through Meta APIs is processed in accordance with Meta's Privacy Policy and Developer Agreements.
  • Trusted Infrastructure Providers: Secure hosting (e.g., AWS/GCP/Vercel) and database infrastructure providers that assist in app operations under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by court order, law enforcement, or applicable legislation to comply with legal processes.

6. Data Storage & Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption in transit using TLS/HTTPS protocols.
  • Encryption at rest for sensitive access tokens and customer collection data.
  • Strict access controls restricting data access to authorized personnel only.
  • Regular security vulnerability audits and monitoring.

7. Data Retention & User Data Deletion Instructions

We retain your data only for as long as your Business Collections account is active or as necessary to provide service.

How to Request Data Deletion (Meta Platform Requirement):

If you wish to remove Business Collections and delete your data from our systems, you can follow these steps:

  1. Log in to your Facebook/Meta account and navigate to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. Select Business Integrations or Apps and Websites.
  3. Locate Business Collections and click Remove.
  4. Alternatively, send an email to support@yourdomain.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" including your Business Account ID.

Upon receiving your request, we will permanently purge all stored access tokens, collection records, and profile details within 30 days, except where retention is legally required.

8. Your Rights & Options

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as GDPR in Europe or CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access & Portability: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request erasure of your data as detailed in Section 7.
  • Revocate Permissions: Disconnect Business Collections from Meta Business Suite at any time via Meta settings.
